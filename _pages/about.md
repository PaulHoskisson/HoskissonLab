Paul A Hoskisson is Professor of Molecular Microbiology at the University of Strathclyde. He was previously the the Royal Academy of Engineering Research Chair in Engineering biology (2020-2025).

Paul completed his first degree at Liverpool John Moores University in 1997 in Applied Microbiology, before a short period in the industry developing Influenza vaccines. He completed his PhD in 2001, studying gentamicin production by the Actinomycete, Micromonospora. Post-doctoral work at the John Innes Centre (Norwich) on Streptomyces developmental biology (2001-2004) and University of Aberdeen, working on bacteriophage defence in Streptomyces (2004-2007) was followed by a move to the University of Strathclyde in 2007 to start his own academic laboratory. 

The research in Paul’s laboratory is focused on evolution and strain improvement of Streptomyces and the evolution of natural product biosynthesis by bacteria.

Paul was elected as a Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh (FRSE) in 2021, he was elected as a member of the Royal Society of Edinburgh Young Academy of Scotland in 2014 and a Fellow of the Royal Society of Biology (FRSB) in 2016. 

Paul is a Senior Editor for Microbial Genomics and an Editor for mBio.

Paul is a member of the REF2029 Panel UoA 5: Biological Sciences.

Paul is a member of the Microbiology Society, the American Society of Microbiology, the Biochemical Society, The Royal Society of Biology, The Society for Molecular Biology and Evolution, The Royal Society of Chemistry and British Herpetological Society. He is the former Editor in Chief of Microbiology Today.

He is the former Chair of Publications Panel and former Co-Chair of the Building Communities Committee at the Microbiology Society. 

He has been a core member of the BBSRC Research Committee D - molecules, Cells and Industrial Biotechnology and remains in the BBSRC Pool of Experts, having served BBSRC Research Committee A, B, C & D and the sLola Panels on an ad hoc basis.
